PDF Toolbox privacy policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

PDF Toolbox ("the app") is a PDF toolkit for Android published by Sahil Gondaliya ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

1. The short version

  • Your PDFs and photos are processed on your device. We do not see them.
  • You do not need an account.
  • A few optional tools use the internet. They run only after you agree, and the files are deleted when the job is done.

2. Files and data on your device

  • The app only reads files you pick (through the Android file picker, camera or share menu). It does not scan your storage.
  • Working copies are kept in the app's temporary cache and removed within 24 hours. Results are saved where you choose (default: Documents/PDF Toolbox).
  • Recent files, favourites, bookmarks, settings and job history are stored locally on your device. Saved signatures are encrypted on your device.
  • Passwords you type to open or protect a PDF are used in memory only and are never stored or logged.

3. Optional cloud features

Some tools need a server (for example Office ↔ PDF conversion, PDF/A, and AI features such as chat with a PDF, summarize and translate). Before the first use, the app shows a consent card.

  • What is sent: the file (or the extracted text, for AI features) you chose, over an encrypted connection, to our backend on Google Firebase / Google Cloud.
  • How it is used: only to produce your result. AI features use Google's Gemini models through Firebase AI Logic.
  • Retention: conversion files are deleted right after the job; an automatic cleanup removes anything left within one hour. We do not use your files to train models.
  • Identifier: to protect the service from abuse, the app signs in anonymously (a random ID, no name or email) and uses Firebase App Check.

4. Diagnostics

If enabled in the release build, the app sends anonymous usage events (for example "a tool was opened") and crash reports through Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. These contain no file names, file contents or passwords. Crash reports may include device model, Android version and the app's state at the time of the crash.

5. Advertising

The app may show ads served by Google AdMob in the future. If ads are shown, Google may use the advertising ID of your device to show and measure ads, and in the EEA/UK we will ask for your consent first. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

6. Permissions

  • Camera – scan documents, ID cards and QR codes (only when you use those tools).
  • Notifications – show progress and results of long tasks.
  • Biometrics – optional App Lock.
  • Internet – optional cloud features, license/consent checks and diagnostics.

7. Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with the service providers needed to run the optional features: Google (Firebase, Google Cloud, AdMob if enabled).

8. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Your choices

You can use nearly all tools offline, decline cloud consent, turn App Lock on or off, and clear app data or uninstall the app at any time to remove everything stored on your device. To ask about data processed by our backend, contact us below.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change and significant changes will be shown in the app.
